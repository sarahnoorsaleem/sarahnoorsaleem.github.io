Current Research

As a Research Assistant at the University of Toronto Mississauga, I collaborate with a multidisciplinary research team investigating perceived and objective walkability among Arab adults in the Greater Toronto Area.

My work includes participant recruitment and engagement, community outreach across the eastern and western Greater Toronto Area, participatory mapping, map digitization in ArcGIS Pro, survey-data preparation, transcription, and mixed-methods research support.

I have digitized more than 30 participant-generated maps to prepare spatial datasets for analysis and contribute to ongoing collaborative research manuscripts.

Graduate Research Direction

My proposed graduate research examines how Pakistani immigrants recreate home and belonging in Mississauga and how their cultural destinations and activity spaces compare with those they experienced in Pakistan.

I am interested in using participatory GIS and qualitative interviews to identify culturally meaningful destinations associated with religion, food, community, recreation, and everyday routines. These locations could be used to reconstruct approximate pre-migration and post-migration activity spaces and examine how everyday places contribute to belonging, cultural continuity, and the recreation of ghar, or home, across places.

This research direction builds on my undergraduate work examining perceived walkability, cultural destinations, and neighbourhood experiences among racialized communities in the Greater Toronto Area.

Selected GIS & Remote Sensing Projects

Selected undergraduate projects demonstrating transportation analysis, spatial accessibility, raster analysis, remote sensing, UAV imagery, and environmental GIS.

Candidate Locations for Future MiWay Transit Service Expansion

Transportation GIS · Network Analysis · Spatial Accessibility

Evaluated potential locations for future MiWay transit expansion using network-based service areas, population density, and land-use characteristics.

Straight-Line vs. Network-Based Transit Service Areas

Network Analysis · Transit Accessibility · GIS

Compared straight-line buffers with network-based service areas around MiWay transit stops to examine differences in estimated accessibility to public transit.

Landsat 8 Land Surface Temperature Analysis

Remote Sensing · Landsat 8 · Thermal Analysis

Processed Landsat 8 thermal imagery to examine spatial variation in land surface temperature and relationships between land cover and thermal conditions.

UAV Orthomosaic from Manual Drone Flight

UAV Remote Sensing · Photogrammetry · Orthomosaic Processing

Processed imagery collected during a manual UAV flight to generate an orthomosaic and examine the spatial coverage of drone imagery.

Cost Surface and Least-Cost Path Analysis

Raster Analysis · Spatial Modelling · Least-Cost Path

Combined terrain and land-cover variables to create a cost surface and identify a least-cost path between selected locations.

Highway 413 and Ontario Greenbelt Intersection

Environmental GIS · Transportation Planning · Cartography

Mapped the proposed Highway 413 alignment in relation to the Ontario Greenbelt to examine areas of geographic intersection and environmental planning concern.
